# Code of conduct

This tracker is used by students, hobbyists, and working scientists. Behave like someone who wants more good physics in the world.

- No harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
- No dumping of other people’s private data, licenses, or unpublished datasets.
- Disagreement about physics is fine. Mocking people for being new is not.
- Keep issues about Beam, GEANT4-as-used-in-Beam, and workflows around the app.

Qirex Scientific OÜ will hide or lock threads that ignore this. Serious abuse can be reported to support@qirexscientific.com.
